# TripEleven — TravelAgency SaaS marketing copy (first person)

## Positioning

We're an **all-in-one, multi-tenant platform for tour & travel agencies** to build, manage, and sell trips online — plus a fully customizable public website template. Every agency gets its own isolated workspace (its own catalog, content, bookings, payments, and branding), behind one shared admin dashboard. We're built for tour operators, trekking companies, DMCs, and boutique travel agencies.

**Our pitch in one line:** *Launch a complete travel agency website — catalog, bookings, payments, SEO, content, and AI trip generation — without writing a line of code.*

## Who it's for

- **Tour & trekking operators** (Nepal/Himalaya focus today, any geography)
- **Destination management companies (DMCs)**
- **Adventure/specialty travel agencies** (jungle safari, rafting, hiking, mountaineering)
- **Travel content creators** who want a bookable website

## Your team, one dashboard

- **Multi-tenant isolation** — every agency's data (trips, media, bookings, customers, content) is fully scoped to its own workspace. No cross-contamination, ever.
- **Staff roles** — Owner, Admin, Manager, Editor — with per-member active/inactive control. Invite your content editor, marketing lead, and booking coordinator into one account with different levels of access.
- **Verification & publishing control** — agencies get verified before publishing trips; a clean DRAFT → PUBLISHED pipeline with pending-approval visibility.

## Trip management — the core

- **An 11-step trip builder**, not a dump of form fields: Basic Info → Trip Facts → Itinerary → Inclusions/Exclusions → Meetings & Drop-offs → Media & Highlights → Pricing → Additional Info → FAQs → SEO → Featured.
- **Multiple itinerary variants** — tabbed variants (Standard / Premium) per trip, each with **drag-and-drop sortable days** (day number, title, description, duration, distance, ascent/descent, meals, accommodation, up to 5 images per day).
- **Rich trip facts** — duration, group size, meals, best seasons, difficulty (Easy/Moderate/Hard/Extreme), transportation, max altitude, accommodation, video intro, locations.
- **Altitude charts** — build elevation profiles from location/altitude points with a **live area-chart preview** (a real differentiator for trekking agencies).
- **Highlights, inclusions, exclusions, packing** — one line per item, clean formatting.
- **FAQ groups** — categorize questions, pick from 31 icons, sortable Q&A pairs.
- **Pricing tiers & featured tags** — multiple price tiers with "Best Value" flags; curated tags (e.g., "Top Rated") to surface your best trips.
- **Status lifecycle** — Draft / Published with editing and deletion; auto-generated **permanent redirects** when a trip's slug changes (no broken links, SEO-safe).
- **Trip slots & departures** — manage start/end dates, capacity, available spots, and per-departure pricing; toggle show/hide on your public site.
- **Featured trips** — hand-curate the trips shown front-and-center on your website.
- **Views & popularity tracking** — real trip views and popularity scores power "most popular" widgets.

## AI trip generation — the wow feature

- **Batch itinerary generation via Claude AI** — type up to 10 topics (one per line), optionally add a focus/style ("luxury, 12–14 days, family-friendly"), and generate full trip itineraries in one batch.
- **Review → Edit → Publish workflow** — each generated item shows its day count, can be checked, retried on failure, edited in the full trip builder, and submitted straight into your catalog.
- Generated output lands directly in the same 11-step builder, so AI drafts are always human-polished.

## Rich content engine — blog + pages

- **Full block-based TipTap editor** — headings, lists, quotes, code blocks (24 languages), resizable tables, text alignment, links, horizontal rules, typography polish, CJK-aware word & character count and **reading time**.
- **Spotlight/focus mode** and a **drag-handle block menu** — turn blocks into other blocks, duplicate, reorder, delete.
- **Slash commands** — type `/` for headings, tables, dividers, and **trip embeds** (`[trip]`), **featured-trip widgets** (`[featured-trips]`), and **post embeds** (`[post]`) that pull live cards into any article.
- **Blog with categories & authors** — cover images, auto-slugs, trash/restore/delete, SEO fields, view & like counters.
- **Info/landing pages** and **legal pages** (privacy/terms) edited in the same editor.
- **Editor templates** — reusable content blocks across trips and pages.
- **Draft autosave** — local autosave so nobody loses work.

## Media library — production-grade

- Central library with **grid search** (by filename, alt, caption), pagination (30/60/90), and multi-file upload.
- **Automatic WebP conversion + compression** on upload (≤1920px, adaptive quality) — fast sites without manual image work.
- **Built-in cropping** — aspect presets (Free, 16:9, 4:3, 3:2, 1:1, 2:3, 3:4, 9:16), zoom, saves a new cropped copy while preserving the original.
- **Alt text / captions / titles** with **metadata baked into the file** (EXIF/IPTC) — accessibility + SEO in one click.
- **Insert from URL**, reusable media picker across the whole dashboard, lazy-loaded thumbnails with shimmer skeletons.

## Bookings & payments — commerce built in

- **Customer booking flow** — travelers register, browse departures, and book trips by departure date + group size.
- **Secure online checkout** — payment-intent checkout with **verification**, and payment confirmation; deposits or full payment.
- **Bookings dashboard** — full lifecycle: Pending payment → Confirmed → Completed / Cancelled / Failed; customer name/email/phone on every booking.
- **Payments dashboard** — amount, type, currency, status, provider, transaction ID, and the linked payment intent. Multi-currency (USD / NPR).
- **Customer accounts** — traveler registration/login, public profiles (username, bio, image), **reviews with ratings** attached to trips, and **wishlists**.

## SEO & growth toolkit — built-in, not bolt-on

- **Per-trip SEO** — meta title, description, keywords, author, canonical, **Open Graph / featured media (1200×630)**, and **JSON-LD structured-data (schema) markup**.
- **Auto slug generation** with **permanent 301 redirects** on slug changes.
- **Redirect manager** with **cycle detection**.
- **Newsletter** — subscriber management with activation/unsubscribe, plus **email blasts** to your list.
- **Reviews & testimonials** — Google & Tripadvisor links, ratings, and counts surfaced on your site; agency-curated testimonials with ratings.
- **Featured tags & curated collections** to drive internal linking and "best of" pages.

## Agency branding & company site

- **Site config** — agency name, logo, established year, contact details, WhatsApp number, full structured address, phone numbers, social links, reviews, and Google Business Profile link.
- **Drag-and-drop menu builder** for navbar and footer — nested dropdowns, indenting, reordering, and even a raw HTML editor for full control.
- **Team & departments** — company org structure (departments, team members with photos, designations, bios).
- **Testimonials, author pages, info pages** — the whole "About us / Why us / Contact" story, editable in the dashboard.
- **PDF itineraries** — branded, print-ready trip PDFs (logo, trip facts, highlights, day-by-day itinerary, inclusions/exclusions) and email delivery to travelers.
- **Email** — transactional and newsletter email via the dashboard.

## The customizable public website — launch-ready

- Our backend exposes a **complete public API** that powers a customer-facing site: published trips with filtering (category/city/region, price range, search), trip detail pages, featured tags, menus, site config, blog, newsletter subscribe/unsubscribe, user accounts, reviews, wishlists, and secure online checkout.
- **The frontend template is our next deliverable** — a ready-to-brand theme with **full customization** (layout, colors, sections) that agencies launch with. Combined with our dashboard's branding tools, it's *"your logo, your site, your bookings — out of the box."*
- Everything is content-driven: your public site is shaped by what you curate in the dashboard (featured trips, nav, footer, testimonials, team, reviews).
